      详细介绍

      PulseRing X1 starts with the board. Our team designed a 6-layer PCB in-house and selected every component for low noise and low standby power. That’s what we mean by high-grade component selection.

      At , PulseRing X1 is rated 5 ATM for everyday water exposure, from hand-washing to the pool. For developers, the charging dock exposes UART and BLE protocol data for low-level debugging. The firmware itself is not open source.
